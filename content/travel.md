## By Car

The Belt Parkway has either not-so-bad days or absolutely-awful-miserable days, and requires you to either take Sunrise Highway or Southern State Parkway. Given the time of year, we'd expect that South Shore beach traffic will be a factor. Fortunately everyone hates North Shore beaches, so you shouldn't have many problems if you can take the Northern State Parkway instead. I would recommend avoiding longer stretches of the Long Island Expressway and transferring to the Northern State if able, though it should not be anywhere as bad as the South Shore.

> [!tip] Parking
> **Valet parking is free of charge**, and there's a self-parking lot immediately next to the venue.

You may also be interested in taking the ferry, which carries cars — see below.

---

## By Ferry

The Bridgeport & Port Jefferson ferry lands 5.5 miles from the Three Village Inn. There is a train station 1.3 miles (uphill) from the Port Jefferson terminal, which can be ridden one stop to the Stony Brook station — itself 1.2 miles from the venue.

The ferry takes both pedestrians and vehicles. I've taken it at least a half-dozen times, and it's a far more enjoyable experience than sitting in traffic. Once you factor in Throgs Neck/Whitestone Bridge tolls and gas, it can be comparatively cost-effective — particularly with carpools — and vehicle tickets purchased in advance are slightly reduced. Schedule and fares are at [pjferry.com/schedule](https://www.pjferry.com/schedule); note that vehicle fares listed are **one-way**.

## By Train

The Stony Brook LIRR station is 1.2 miles from the venue, on the **Huntington/Port Jefferson line**. I've traveled this dozens of times: take the subway to Jamaica or Woodside first, or ride the LIRR straight out of Penn Station with a transfer at Huntington. Coming from Grand Central on Metro-North? Either walk over to Penn or hop the 7 train to Woodside. Amtrak's Northeast Regional runs to Penn Station, and the same LIRR connection (transfer at Huntington) works from there.

## By Plane

**Long Island MacArthur (ISP)** is approximately 30 minutes away, with nonstop flights from Boston, Tampa, Orlando, Charlotte, and more. If you're coming from out of state, it may be the most convenient choice of all — it skips every ounce of NYC-related traffic. Even a sub-two-hour layover to catch a connection into ISP can be worth it.

**LaGuardia (LGA)** is a lot easier to get out of than the other city airports, has the shortest travel time to Stony Brook among them, and is a much better-run experience all around now that the renovations are complete. **Westchester County (HPN)** is also worth considering, though it adds ~30–40 minutes versus LGA or JFK. We would *not* recommend flying into **Newark (EWR)**.
