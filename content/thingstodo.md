## Stony Brook/The Three Villages

Of course, one thing to do at Stony Brook is tour the Stony Brook University campus. [The Wang Center](https://www.stonybrook.edu/commcms/wang/) hosts art and culture exhibitions with an eatery located inside. Visitors can purchase a 24-hour subscription with a credit or debit card to [Wolf Ride Bike Share](https://www.stonybrook.edu/mobility-and-parking/transportation/sustainability/bike-share.html): a self-serve bike rental program with stations all around campus. The Paul Simons Memorial Bike Path circles the entire campus, if you are looking to get a quick workout in. For last minute shopping, [Smith Haven Mall](https://www.simon.com/mall/smith-haven-mall) has plenty of stores to peruse. [The Jazz Loft](https://thejazzloft.org/), the [Vanderbilt Museum and Planetarium](https://www.vanderbiltmuseum.org/), or a [Washington Spy Trail Tour](https://www.culper.com/contact/) may be of interest to you. 

[West Meadow Beach](https://www.brookhavenny.gov/Facilities/Facility/Details/West-Meadow-Beach-7), [Avalon Nature Preserve](https://avalonnaturepreserve.org), and [Sunken Meadow State Park](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/state-parks/sunken-meadow-state-park-governor-alfred-e-smith) are perfect for taking in Long Island's beautiful and lesser-known nature and views. Please be advised that the north shore beaches are rocky, while the south shore beaches are more appropriate for swimming. To that end, [Jones Beach](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/state-parks/jones-beach-state-park), [Gilgo Beach](https://townofbabylonny.gov/438/Gilgo-Beach), and [Robert Moses State Park](https://parks.ny.gov/visit/state-parks/robert-moses-state-park-long-island) are all within an hour's drive of Stony Brook. 

[The Halal Hut](https://maps.app.goo.gl/iVyJZUk4L4PcsaVu6) and [Green Tea](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VLQvbrypD4stiaw8A) are popular spots within walking distance of the university. Marisa was also a frequent patron of the [Kung Fu Tea](https://maps.app.goo.gl/evHypPZWSRzxYyTA6). With access to a car, [Ichi Sushi & Ramen](https://www.ichisushiramen.com/menu/), [Red Tiger Dumpling House](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3ijKE31VHDEWZyH78), and [Casa Luis](https://maps.app.goo.gl/quav5kPE6StoUeQf8) are some of our favorites. If you didn't have time to stop by Brooklyn on the drive over, [Strathmore Bagels](https://www.strathmorebagelsstonybrook.com/) and [Se-port Delicatessen](http://se-portdeli.com/) are just as good!

## Port Jefferson

East of Stony Brook is Port Jefferson: a village overlooking the harbor and Long Island Sound. Along Main Street and Broadway, there are many locally owned shops, cafes, and restaurants to explore. [Slurp Ramen](https://www.slurpusa.com/) is a favorite among all Stony Brook students, while the [Curry Club](https://maps.app.goo.gl/H779cWpjqZwMykKa7) is a more upscale date night spot. Many restaurants play live music in the evenings as well. 

The Port Jefferson train station is one stop away from Stony Brook, but is unfortunately a 30-minute roadside walk from the harborfront area. Within a 15-minute walk of the train station is [Grumpy Jack's](https://www.grumpyjacksportjeff.com/) (marinated burger is a must-try) and [Toast Coffee + Kitchen](https://www.toastcoffeehouse.com/location/toast-coffeehouse-port-jefferson/) (reservations recommended to try some of the best coffee in Suffolk county!). [Colosseo Pizza](https://maps.app.goo.gl/bL2wgHmcCPwmQat89) is Max's favorite pizza from our college days). For groceries, we recommend [Uncle Giuseppe's Marketplace](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ycEhhETgFWYMyLY8A).
