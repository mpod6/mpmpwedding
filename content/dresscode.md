Our dress code is [semi-formal](https://www.outfiten.com/what-is-the-semi-formal-dress-code-and-what-to-wear/). As our website theme shows, our wedding colors are maroon and olive green!

At Stony Brook, August temperatures range between 71 and 90 degrees Fahrenheit with low to moderate humidity. The Three Village Inn parking lot is paved, and there will also be a complementary valet. The ceremony will be held outdoors on a partially paved, partially shaded grassy lawn. In the event of rain, the ceremony will be held indoors. The cocktail hour will be held indoors with the option to sit out on the patio. The reception and afterparty will be held indoors.
